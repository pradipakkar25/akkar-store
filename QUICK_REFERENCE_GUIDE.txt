PRODUCT QR CODE CHECKOUT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
How It Works:
1. Click "📱 Scan QR" on any product
2. QR code appears (150x150 pixels)
3. Scan with phone camera
4. Automatically redirected to checkout
5. Product added to cart
6. Price pre-filled from product
7. Complete payment with UPI (9923554590)

QR Code Encoding:
/product-checkout?id=[PRODUCT_ID]&name=[NAME]&price=[PRICE]
